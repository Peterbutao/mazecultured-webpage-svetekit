Jeze is a photo series exploring the intersection of football and fashion — two cultures that have always shared a strong influence on how we express ourselves.

Through the jerseys we wear, the clubs we represent, and the way we style them, Jeze looks beyond the pitch to celebrate football as a part of everyday culture. The series primarily sources its jerseys through DAPP, giving pre-loved pieces a new life while highlighting the stories, identity and style attached to them.

More than just football shirts, Jeze is about the people, the culture and the fashion that make the beautiful game what it is.
